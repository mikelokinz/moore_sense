Physical Telemetry Parameters & Sensor Specifications
The INCOIS-NIOT OMNI (Ocean Moored Buoy Network in the Northern Indian Ocean) collects real-time meteorological, ocean surface, and wave measurements. Here is what each pulled metric represents:
Air Temperature (`air_temperature`) °C
Ambient atmospheric dry-bulb temperature measured by an aspirated platinum resistance thermometer (PRT) mounted on the buoy mast at 3 meters above the mean waterline.
Crucial for air-sea heat flux and atmospheric stability computations.
Atmospheric Pressure (`air_pressure`) hPa / mbar
Barometric pressure reduced to mean sea level measured by a high-precision digital silicon resonance barometer inside the meteorological payload.
Essential for tracking monsoons, deep depressions, and tropical cyclogenesis.
Significant Wave Height (`hm0`) m
Calculated as four times the square root of the zeroth moment of the wave spectrum (Hm0 ≈ 4√m0) using high-precision 3-axis solid-state accelerometers and MRU.
Directly tracks ocean swell, sea state roughness, and marine navigational hazards.
Relative Humidity (`humidity`) %
Marine boundary layer relative humidity measured by a capacitive thin-film polymer sensor housed in a radiation shield at 3m mast elevation.
Used with temperature to compute marine dew point and latent heat transfer.
Sea Surface Temperature (`sst`) °C
Near-surface bulk ocean temperature measured at 1 meter depth below water line on the mooring bridle.
Indicates ocean thermal heat content feeding tropical storms.
Public Access Restrictions Note Policy
On certain public INCOIS portals, surface wind speed/direction downloads on select EEZ stations (e.g. AD09, AD10, BD12) return RESTRICTED to protect sovereign EEZ agreements. The system respects these flags without inventing dummy data.
